Your Role in Global Pollution

While global pollution may seem like a large-scale issue, everyday human activities contribute significantly to environmental damage. From the way we travel to the energy we consume and the food we eat, our daily choices leave a measurable impact on the planet.

One of the most effective ways to understand this impact is by calculating your carbon footprint. A carbon footprint represents the total amount of greenhouse gases—especially carbon dioxide—generated by your actions.

Why Calculate Your Carbon Footprint?

  • Understand how your lifestyle contributes to climate change
  • Identify areas where you can reduce emissions
  • Make more sustainable choices in transportation, energy use, and consumption
  • Support global efforts to reduce environmental damage
