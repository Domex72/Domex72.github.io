New Biofilter Living Wall Proposed

Exciting developments are underway as community members propose an active biofilter living wall installation at the Royal Ontario Museum. This initiative aims to improve indoor air quality and showcase urban ecological design right in the heart of the biodiversity exhibition.

Advocating for Water Protection

Ongoing efforts to secure environmental justice and protect vital waterbodies remain a top priority. Community leaders are currently focusing on the protection of vital areas, emphasizing the importance of respecting indigenous treaties and preserving these ecosystems.
